**Promotional text** (170):
Clean, ad-free weather. Current conditions, hourly and 7-day forecasts, live radar, National Weather Service alerts and hurricane tracking in one calm screen.

**Description** (4000):
Weather Bear is weather without the clutter. No ads, no account, no tracking. Open it and the weather is just there.

TODAY AT A GLANCE
Current temperature, feels-like, wind and humidity, with sunrise and sunset right underneath.

HOURLY AND 7-DAY
Scroll the next hours with rain chances, then see the week ahead with highs, lows and a range bar that shows how each day compares.

LIVE RADAR
Watch the last hour of rain and storms move across the map.

SEVERE WEATHER ALERTS
Watches, warnings and advisories from the U.S. National Weather Service for the place you're looking at, color-coded by severity.

TROPICAL STORMS
Active storms and the tropical outlook from the National Hurricane Center during hurricane season.

WIDGETS
Put the temperature, conditions and today's high and low on your Home Screen or Lock Screen.

ANY PLACE
Use your location or search for any city.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Weather Bear has no ads, no analytics and no server of its own. Your location is used only to fetch the weather and never leaves your device except in those weather requests.

Forecast data by Open-Meteo. Alerts by the National Weather Service. Radar by RainViewer. Maps by OpenStreetMap contributors.

## App Privacy ("nutrition label") answers
- Data collection: **Yes**, one type, to be conservative: **Location › Precise Location**
  (coordinates go to Open-Meteo and NWS to fetch the forecast).
  - Use: App Functionality only
  - Linked to the user's identity: No
  - Used for tracking: No
- Nothing else is collected (no contact info, identifiers, usage data or diagnostics).

## App Review notes (paste into "Notes" for the reviewer)
Weather Bear needs no sign-in. Allow location, or tap the place name and search any city.
Native features: iOS location services and Home Screen / Lock Screen widgets (WidgetKit). Weather data comes from public government and
open-data services; the app has no server or account system.

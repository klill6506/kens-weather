Get help or report a problem

Open an issue at github.com/klill6506/kens-weather/issues and describe what you saw, the place you were looking at, and your iPhone model.

Common questions

The app shows the wrong place. Tap the place name at the top and search for your city, or tap the location button to use where you are.

Location doesn't work. Go to Settings › Weather Bear › Location and choose While Using the App.

Where does the weather come from? Forecasts come from Open-Meteo, alerts from the U.S. National Weather Service, radar from RainViewer, and tropical storms from the National Hurricane Center.
